The simple plate formula for better meals

A flexible way to balance your meals without weighing every ingredient or banning the foods you love.

VSBy Vignesh Sivasankaran
Weight-loss coach · 19 March 2025
02BUILD
YOUR
PLATE

Nutrition becomes much easier when you stop treating every meal like a maths test. A simple visual guide can help you build satisfying meals while leaving room for culture, preferences, and real life.

Start with a palm of protein

Protein helps meals feel satisfying and supports your body while you lose weight. Try eggs, chicken, fish, tofu, paneer, Greek yogurt, beans, or lentils. One palm-sized serving is a useful starting point.

Fill half with colour

Vegetables and fruit bring volume, fibre, and variety. Aim for roughly half your plate from colourful plants when it suits the meal. Fresh, frozen, and cooked all count.

Add a fist of carbohydrates

Rice, potatoes, oats, bread, and other grains can absolutely fit. Choose a portion around the size of your fist, adjusting for hunger and activity. Carbs are fuel—not a moral test.

Finish with flavour

A thumb of fats, a sauce you enjoy, or a little dessert can make a meal feel complete. Sustainable eating includes pleasure. The goal is a pattern you can repeat, not a plate that looks perfect every time.
